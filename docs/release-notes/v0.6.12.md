# OneCode 0.6.12 Beta candidate

Compact & Resume could lose local tool authorization twenty minutes after its handoff committed. Cleanup removed the completed transaction even though the successor response still used that exact receipt. Completed handoffs now retain their durable task proof through cleanup and restart, independently of the recovery budget for unfinished transactions. Tool admission continues to require the current chat attachment, exact native question and original response; stopped or replaced tasks cannot borrow retained receipts.

Includes the 0.6.11 early Steer and 0.6.10 follow-up repairs. Recorder revision 75 and bridge protocol 19 are unchanged. Beta acceptance continues on Windows and native Ubuntu, including the internal capsule updater and the Companion waiting-for-local-tools case. Stable promotion remains gated on acceptance. No production task is replayed or restarted.
