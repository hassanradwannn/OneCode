# OneCode 0.6.16 Beta candidate

Capsule-only Beta acceptance update for compatible existing Beta installations. The installed updater verifies the complete-base binding and applies the small application capsule; this release does not publish a replacement installer or AppImage.

Native Windows acceptance still found roughly two minutes of Steer delay in 0.6.15. The provider had already restored its ready composer while its original local poll continued, but cancellation confirmation remained tied to a missing response-body or terminal-item receipt.

Explicit Steer now captures the exact native primary Stop before its authorized click and can witness that same editor and form changing to the enabled native Voice control. It retains the original question, conversation, response and lease checks. A pre-existing idle page, changed question, draft, attachment, approval, disabled or ambiguous control, or replacement editor cannot authorize this transition. Automatic Continue and Goal/Loop retain their existing terminal-proof rules. Steer does not stop or replay local work.

Bridge protocol 19 is unchanged; recorder revision 77 loads the updated adapter and observer together. Windows and native Ubuntu Beta acceptance remains in progress. The previously identified IPC integration failures also remain open for stable signoff; this candidate does not claim a complete acceptance pass.
