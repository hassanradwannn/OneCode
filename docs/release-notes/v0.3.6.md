# OneCode 0.3.6

## What's new

- Improved **Compact & Resume** for ChatGPT and Gemini.
- Smoother **computer use and screenshots**.
- More consistent **tool confirmations** and chat status.
- Cleaner progress messages after tasks finish.
- An optional **ChatGPT skill** that reduces repeated instructions.

## Update and setup

Update from **Settings → General → Check for updates**, or [download the Windows installer](https://github.com/hassanradwannn/OneCode/releases/download/v0.3.600/OneCode-Setup-x64.exe). Your saved chats and settings are preserved.

For a new installation, follow the [setup guide](https://github.com/hassanradwannn/OneCode/blob/main/docs/WINDOWS-SETUP.md) to connect your browser and chosen provider. The matching Companion extension is included below.

To use the optional ChatGPT skill, save its ZIP from **OneCode Setup**, upload and enable it in ChatGPT, then enable the matching OneCode option. Leave it off if your ChatGPT account does not support Skills.
