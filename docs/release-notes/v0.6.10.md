Exact native interruption can confirm continuation Stop without a final assistant message. Native question, draft, refresh, Send and tool ownership checks remain. Steer preserves local processes; explicit Stop retains its separate exact-session termination path. Generated checkpoints, scheduled checks and automatic Continue still wait for settled work.

Recorder revision 74 and bridge protocol 19 are unchanged from the earlier candidate. Version 0.6.9 remains an immutable superseded draft; it is not rebuilt or promoted. Native Windows/Linux, ChatGPT/Gemini, Desktop/Remote and both installed updater lanes remain release gates. Stable remains unchanged, and the overnight Windows crash cause remains unproved.
